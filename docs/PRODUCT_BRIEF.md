# HATUA product brief

**Updated:** 2026-10-18. **Stage:** exploratory prototype, not launched.

## Problem

People in Kenya facing financial hardship often struggle to discover legitimate entry-level jobs, local training, financial support programmes, and trustworthy contacts. People seeking help for alcohol or other substance-use concerns may likewise lack a discreet, accessible way to locate established qualified services. These are related social challenges but separate user needs.

## Mission

Enable a person to **find a trustworthy next step in minutes**: discover a verified opportunity, contact a support resource, or complete a self-chosen plan action, even with a low-cost Android phone.

## Audiences

- Individual adults seeking jobs, apprenticeships, skills and trustworthy programme information.
- Individuals voluntarily seeking substance-use support information, counselling referrals or verified provider contacts.
- Future: vetted NGOs, providers and community-programme coordinators as paying operational customers.

No automatic sharing of addiction-related information with employers or opportunity systems. No paid essential help.

## Value propositions

1. Discover verified public opportunities without creating an account or paying.
2. Quickly find public contact information for qualified support, starting with NACADA 1192 and a reviewed directory.
3. Privately turn a goal into tangible next steps.
4. Partner programme teams (future) coordinate referrals and report **consented**, privacy-safe outcomes.

## Primary success metric

**Verified next-step action rate:** proportion of users who use a legitimate official application/contact route or complete a useful self-chosen task, not raw downloads or chatbot messages. Instrument only with proportionate consent and privacy safeguards; don't collect sensitive health events by default.

## Non-goals for first release

- Diagnosing/treating addiction; automated detox instructions; real-time crisis counselling.
- Job placement guarantees, loan underwriting, lending or recruiting fees.
- Public recovery social network, advertising/profiling users with health data.
- Opening bookings at unaffiliated clinics or submitting applications without authorised integrations.

## Kenya-first assumptions to validate

- The 1192 contact is appropriate for alcohol and substance-use support; verify the current service details with NACADA before release.
- Partner NGOs have funds and operational needs; interviews are required, not assumed.
- Language at public launch should include English and Kiswahili.
- Users need low data cost, clear official source links and reliable offline public reference content.
