# Sustainable business model and validation

## Principle

Free public support and opportunities. Organisations, not vulnerable individuals, pay for software that helps coordinate legitimate programmes and referrals.

## Candidate payers

- NGOs organising training and employability programmes.
- Community groups managing outreach and verified service directories.
- Licensed treatment/referral partners, only after appropriate legal/clinical safeguards.
- CSR programmes, foundations and funders needing aggregate, privacy-protecting monitoring.

## Proposed, unvalidated tiers

- Individuals: FREE.
- Community Partner: KSh 4,999/month (programme listings, limited partner tools once shipped).
- Programme Partner: KSh 14,999/month (staff/workflows/aggregate reporting once shipped).
- Custom implementations and staff onboarding: contract-based.

These amounts are pricing **hypotheses** and no partner has committed to pay. Do not create payment screens before verifying willingness to pay, costs and deliverable features. If zero customers pay, monthly revenue is zero.

## Validation plan (before partner build)

1. Interview at least 10 NGOs/community programme coordinators and at least 15 prospective end users across target groups, with appropriate safeguarding.
2. Observe actual problems in referral management, verified outreach and opportunity applications.
3. Prototype one workflow with no sensitive health record capture.
4. Ask three qualified organisations for letters of intent or paid pilot commitments with concrete deliverables.
5. Test whether referral outcomes and programme steps can be measured ethically, without collecting unnecessary personal data.
6. Model support/training cost, organisation churn, conversion and hosting budgets.

## Avoid conflicts of interest

Do not sell priority recovery referrals, suppress free alternatives, charge for government programmes, infer eligibility without evidence, or disclose health activity to prospective employers. Sponsorship, if ever introduced, must be clearly distinguished from objective recommendations.
